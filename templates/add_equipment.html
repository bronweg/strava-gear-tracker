How it works

Once you add equipment, the app will automatically fetch your outdoor Strava rides (Ride and EBikeRide activities) from the installation date until today and calculate the total distance and time you've ridden with this equipment. Virtual/indoor rides are excluded since they don't cause wear on outdoor bicycle components.

Bike-Specific Tracking: If you select a specific bike, only activities recorded with that bike will be counted. This is useful for bike-specific components like chains, cassettes, or tires. For equipment used across multiple bikes (like pedals or a saddle), leave the bike selection as "All bikes".
